5SOS Can't Stop Winning Boyband of the Year

Listen Now

Ladies and gentlemen, history is happening, and it’s happening in a very large arena. 5 Seconds of Summer have been at it since 2011. Luke, Michael, Calum, and Ashton came up from Sydney, took over the world, and then did the one thing a boyband is never supposed to do: they didn’t break up. They came back, bringing bigger shows, a bigger budget, and a much bigger opinion of themselves.

Everyone’s a Star! is the official record of their return, one marked with a suspicious number of “Boyband of the Year” wins. That’s the title they’ve given themselves, and the album does everything it can to make you agree.

You can follow along as they make history, one legendary stop at a time. Or you can just listen to the songs, which are loud and catchy enough to back up every bit of the hype.

Everyone’s a star, but we all know who’s the biggest.
